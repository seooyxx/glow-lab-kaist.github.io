If you are interested in joining the lab, send an email to seung.new.email@gmail.com that includes:

How to apply

  • Your CV
  • Your academic transcript — an unofficial copy is fine
  • A brief research statement — the research you would like to pursue, and how it aligns with the lab's interests and the PI's past work

Due to the volume of emails, we may not be able to respond to every message — but every application is read.

What we look for

Prior experience on closely related topics is not a strict prerequisite. We look for highly motivated students with strong and interesting prior experiences — research projects, open-source contributions, internships, or other evidence of initiative and curiosity.
